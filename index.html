Who we are

About Us

CARINGLY connects individuals, families and communities with compassionate care, counselling, legal support, mediation and trusted donors.

Our mission

To connect individuals, families and communities in need with compassionate care, professional counselling, legal support, mediation and verified donors, through ethical, evidence-based social work practice.

Our vision

A caring society where no one faces hardship alone, and every person can access support, dignity and hope.

Our team

Meet our team

The professionals behind our care connection, counselling and support groups.

Dr. Lanre-Babalola Folake Olubunmi

Co-Founder · Associate Professor · Certified Social Worker

Dr. Folake helps people find the right support at the right time, from young people finding their footing to families and older adults navigating life’s transitions. Her work bridges clinical care and community support through research, advocacy and compassionate service.

Her focus includes care navigation, youth and adolescent support, gerontological social work, psychosocial counselling and retirement transitions. She holds a B.Sc., M.Sc. and PhD in Sociology, an M.Sc. in Social Work and an MBA, and is completing a Master’s in Environmental Health Management.

Prof. Hassan Adedoyin

Director of Strategy, Governance and Resource Mobilisation

He holds a PhD in Business Administration and is a Professor of International Business Management at Conestoga College, Ontario. Principles need systems to carry them. Drawing on his expertise as a Professor of Business Administration, Prof. Hassan Adedoyin steers CARINGLY’s strategy, governance and financial controls, and leads our efforts to attract funding with the transparency that donors and partners expect.

Dr. Adeoluwa Toluwa Adeniyi

Head of Social Relations and Mediation

Dr. Adeoluwa holds a PhD in Sociology and specialises in how people live, disagree, heal and reconnect. He leads CARINGLY’s mediation service and support groups, and tracks what works to help improve our services.

Fagbamila Godable (Able International)

Digital Engagement and Community Manager

Godable builds online communities where people feel seen. He manages CARINGLY’s social media channels and awareness campaigns, turning followers into volunteers, donors and advocates.

Obinduro Amaka O.

Youth Engagement and Community Outreach Officer

Amaka is a trained social worker who takes support into schools, churches, mosques, prisons and neighbourhoods. She plans CARINGLY’s outreaches, mobilises volunteers, and helps young people find their voice and their next step.

Prof. Ogunbameru Olakunle A.

Senior Adviser, Research and Ethics

A retired professor of sociology and author of several books, including works on preparing for retirement and retiring well, Prof. Ogunbameru has spent decades studying how society can best care for its people. At CARINGLY, he brings his scholarship to our research and ethics, supporting rigorous, evidence-based work that treats everyone with dignity. His expertise also adds depth to our ageing well programmes.

Our goals

Objectives

  1. 1Identify and assess vulnerable persons and link them to suitable care providers and services.
  2. 2Provide accessible, confidential and professional counselling for individuals, families and groups.
  3. 3Offer legal support and guidance on care, family welfare and the rights of vulnerable persons.
  4. 4Provide mediating services to resolve family, community and care-related conflicts peacefully.
  5. 5Mobilise and match donors, volunteers and partners to verified needs.
  6. 6Ensure transparency and accountability in all donations and referrals.
  7. 7Build partnerships with hospitals, faith bodies, schools, government and community groups.
  8. 8Promote awareness, advocacy and training on care, wellbeing and social justice.
  9. 9Follow up every case to measure outcomes and keep improving our services.
